        Before you apply: checklist

        • Read the RHP: business, risks, objects of the issue
        • Fresh issue versus offer for sale (OFS)
        • Revenue, profit and debt trend; valuation versus peers
        • Promoter background and pledge
        • Subscription by QIB, NII and retail on day 3
        • GMP trend, not a single day's number
        • SME IPOs: large lot sizes, low liquidity, higher risk
        • Apply through UPI; keep the mandate approved

            Read first: India rules

            Resident Indians may trade currency derivatives on NSE and BSE (USD/INR, EUR/INR, GBP/INR, JPY/INR) through SEBI-registered brokers. Offshore forex platforms are not permitted under FEMA, and RBI maintains an Alert List. Verify before you deposit.
